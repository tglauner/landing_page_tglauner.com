About

Practical finance education with quantitative depth.

TG Investments and Research provides financial education and open-source technology tools for retail and professional investors. Founder Tim Glauner brings more than 30 years of capital markets experience across New York, London, and Paris, with a background spanning quantitative finance, regulatory frameworks, and structured products.

The material is written for people who expect precision: senior traders, structurers, model validators, risk managers, quant developers, and unusually strong learners who want the mechanics behind the desk conversation.

Markets
